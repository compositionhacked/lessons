# Lesson 31 — Delay the resolution.

**Album study:** Dire Straits, *Communiqué* (1979) · **Song reference:** “Where Do You Think You're Going?” · **Tempo:** 72 BPM · **Meter:** 4/4 · **Tuning:** standard guitar tuning.

## The device

Resolution has pitch, rhythm and register. Hold two steady; delay one. Here the dominant G7 prepares C, but the phrase withholds the tonic until the final attack.

## Eight-bar harmony

Cm · Abmaj7 · Fm · G7 · Cm · Abmaj7 · Fm · G7

## Writing assignment

Write a two-bar question ending on an incomplete chord tone. Repeat it, then delay the tonic by one subdivision while keeping the pulse clear.
