# Lesson 027 · Bend by different amounts

[Download MIDI](027-demonstration.mid) · [Download MP3](027-demonstration.mp3)

84 BPM · 4/4

On Am, bend B4→C5 by one semitone and D5→E5 by two. On Dm, bend C5→D5 by two and E5→F5 by one. In standard tuning, play the lower voice on the B string (12b13, 13b15) and the higher on the high E (10b12, 12b13). Match arrival times. Set MIDI bend range to ±2 semitones.

Import the MIDI into Ableton at 84 BPM and assign instruments.

## Writing exercise

Write eight bars over Am | Dm | E | Am twice. Use unequal bends on Am and Dm. Record two takes and check their landing pitches.
