# Lesson 007 — Make bass and treble take turns

Original music and synthesized teaching audio. 120 BPM, 4/4 shuffle, standard tuning, concert pitch.

[MP3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/compositionhacked/lessons/main/007-make-bass-and-treble-take-turns/007-demonstration.mp3) · [MIDI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/compositionhacked/lessons/main/007-make-bass-and-treble-take-turns/007-demonstration.mid)

Hear the aligned parts, then the same notes with treble attacks moved to the late triplet. The bass plays E2–B2–C-sharp3–B2 on the beats. The upper E7 fragment is G-sharp3–D4–E4. Both keep four attacks per bar. No continuous low drone is added.

Write eight bars: E7–E7–A7–A7 | E7–A7–B7–E7. Keep bass on beats and treble on late triplets. The Reel includes a four-bar preview, not the full assignment.

Adapted from Lesson 1 of Fabio Noronha’s Texas Flood composition method. An original teaching example, not an SRV transcription or artist recording. Virtual music-history study: Austin, Texas, where Vaughan developed his career; not an album recording-location claim.
