# Lesson 007 — Make bass and treble take turns

Original music and synthesized teaching audio. 120 BPM, 4/4, concert pitch, standard tuning E2 A2 D3 G3 B3 E4. C4 = MIDI 60. No Stevie Ray Vaughan recording or transcription is distributed here.

[Download MP3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/compositionhacked/lessons/main/007-make-bass-and-treble-take-turns/007-demonstration.mp3) · [Download editable MIDI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/compositionhacked/lessons/main/007-make-bass-and-treble-take-turns/007-demonstration.mid) · [Reel MP4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/compositionhacked/lessons/main/007-make-bass-and-treble-take-turns/007-reel.mp4)

## Hear and understand

The bass attacks E2–B2–C♯3–B2 on beats 1, 2, 3, 4. The treble is the E7 fragment G♯3–D4–E4. In A both attack together. In B each treble attack is shifted later by 2/3 of a beat (1/3 second at 120 BPM). Both parts retain four attacks per bar, their original pitches, lengths, velocities and timbre. Bass lasts 0.255 s; each treble note lasts 0.120 s. There is no low drone.

Triplet grid: bass slots 1,4,7,10; B treble slots 3,6,9,12. The bass and treble are separately editable tracks. A quiet quarter-note hi-hat is constant. All synthesis is generated from the exported event list; the MIDI uses GM instrument suggestions, so your DAW's timbre will differ.

| Chord | Bass root–5–6–5 | Treble fragment | Standard guitar, low to high |
|---|---|---|---|
| E7 | E2–B2–C♯3–B2 | G♯3–D4–E4 | x x x 1 3 0 |
| A7 | A2–E3–F♯3–E3 | G3–C♯4–E4 | x x x 0 2 0 |
| B7 | B2–F♯3–G♯3–F♯3 | A3–D♯4–F♯4 | x x x 2 4 2 |

Bass locations: E2 low E open, B2 A2, C♯3 A4; A2 A open, E3 D2, F♯3 D4; B2 A2, F♯3 D4, G♯3 D6. Here A2 in a location means A string fret 2, not an octave label. Muting is essential. The E bass and C♯ bass notes briefly imply different colours under a repeated upper fragment; E7 names the overall dominant field rather than claiming every bass beat is a block root-position chord.

## Write your version

Write eight bars, one chord per bar: **E7–E7–A7–A7 | E7–A7–B7–E7**. Keep bass on quarter-note beats and treble on late triplets. Use the root–5–6–5 bass patterns above. Do not merge attacks except for a separate ending after bar 8. Record an aligned A version and an interlocking B version at identical settings. The downloadable 24-bar demonstration includes a four-bar preview, not a completed eight-bar assignment.

## Play or edit in a DAW

Listen before opening the MIDI. Import the Standard MIDI File with its 120 BPM tempo map. Assign a bass sound to the bass track, clean guitar or another short plucked sound to the upper/lead track, and a GM-compatible drum kit to channel 10. Keep the notes and velocities at first; different instruments will change the sound. Disable quantization that would move triplets onto straight eighths. Loop a four-bar comparison, then alter only the stated variable. Compare at a fixed master gain. The JSON score provides exact event times and MIDI pitches. All note-offs stop at the intended end; no sustained low replacement is hidden in the audio.
