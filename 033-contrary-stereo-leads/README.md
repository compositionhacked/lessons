# Lesson 033 · Split two leads in opposite directions

[Download MIDI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/compositionhacked/lessons/main/033-contrary-stereo-leads/033-demonstration.mid) · [Download MP3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/compositionhacked/lessons/main/033-contrary-stereo-leads/033-demonstration.mp3)

180 BPM · 4/4

At 180 BPM, pan two leads left and right. Before the split, both play C4–D4–E4–F4 in parallel octaves. In the split, start on C4/C5. The left part descends C4–B♭3–A3–G3–F3–E3–D3–C3 while the right ascends C5–D5–E5–F5–G5–A5–B♭5–C6. Reverse to close the gap, then land on F3/F4.

In Ableton Live, set the project tempo to 180 BPM, then drag in the MIDI and assign instruments. Listen to the MP3 before editing.

## Writing exercise

Write eight bars in F major. Double a motif in octaves for two bars, diverge in contrary motion for two, reverse for two, then converge on F.
