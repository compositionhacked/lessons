# Lesson 008 — Make an eight-slot riff cross the barline

Original music and synthesized teaching audio. 120 BPM, 4/4, concert pitch, standard tuning E2 A2 D3 G3 B3 E4. C4 = MIDI 60. No Stevie Ray Vaughan recording or transcription is distributed here.

## Hear and understand

At 120 BPM each quarter note is 0.5 s and each triplet slot is 1/6 s. A bar is 12 slots. The cell is eight slots long: attack E4 (MIDI 64) at relative slot 0, G4 (67) at 2, A4 (69) at 3, B4 (71) at 5. All four notes last 0.115 s. E4 velocity is 105; the other notes are 78. Rest at slots 1,4,6,7.

In A the cell restarts every 12 slots, with four slots of space after each cell. In B it restarts every eight slots. Its starts are at absolute slots 0,8,16,24: bar 1 beat 1; bar 1 late triplet of beat 3; bar 2 middle triplet of beat 2; bar 3 beat 1. Three eight-slot cells fill two twelve-slot bars, so downbeats realign every two bars. B has six cell statements per four bars; A has four. The density change is intentional and identified in the Reel and caption.

The E7 accompaniment stays identical throughout: E2 (40) on each quarter note, upper G♯3 (56) and D4 (62) on beats 1 and 3, quiet quarter-note hi-hat. Bass duration 0.260 s, upper-shell duration 0.180 s. E7 is a shell with its fifth omitted, not a complete triad. The bass follows the bar, not the cell.

Standard-tuning melody: high e open, fret 3, fret 5, fret 7 = E4, G4, A4, B4. In MIDI, one slot is 320 ticks at 960 PPQ; the cell is 2560 ticks and the bar is 3840. The exported MIDI contains conductor markers, bass, harmony, lead and pulse. The MP3 uses the same events with original plucked synthesis; GM instrument suggestions will sound different in your DAW.

## Demonstration timing

The MP3, MIDI and 48-second Reel represent the same 24 bars.

| Seconds | Section |
|---|---|
| 0–8 | A, four bars with a reset each bar |
| 8–16 | B, continuous eight-slot cells |
| 16–24 | B, count the eight-slot cell |
| 24–32 | B, hear the migrating start |
| 32–40 | A replay, fewer cell statements |
| 40–48 | B loop under the writing brief |

## Write your version

Write eight bars on **E7**. Loop the cell across bars 1–6; deliberately truncate it in bar 7 and land on E4 at bar 8 beat 1. Keep the bass and pulse aligned to the bar. Annotate every cell start and make E4 audibly stronger. The demonstration loops A and B for teaching; it does not contain the completed eight-bar ending you are assigned to compose.

## Play or edit in a DAW

Listen before opening the MIDI. Import the Standard MIDI File with its 120 BPM tempo map. Assign a bass sound to the bass track, clean guitar or another short plucked sound to the upper/lead track, and a GM-compatible drum kit to channel 10. Keep the notes and velocities at first; different instruments will change the sound. Disable quantization that would move triplets onto straight eighths. Loop a four-bar comparison, then alter only the stated variable. Compare at a fixed master gain. The JSON score provides exact event times and MIDI pitches. All note-offs stop at the intended end; no sustained low replacement is hidden in the audio.
