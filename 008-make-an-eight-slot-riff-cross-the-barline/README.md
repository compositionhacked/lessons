# Lesson 008 — Make an eight-slot riff cross the barline

An original 120 BPM, 4/4 triplet-grid composition study. Cell attacks: E4 at slot 0, G4 at 2, A4 at 3, B4 at 5. The cell lasts eight slots; each bar lasts twelve. Three cells fill two bars.

A resets each bar; B loops continuously. B intentionally has more cell statements. E7 backing and quarter-note E2 bass stay fixed.

Write eight bars on E7: loop in bars 1–6, cut in bar 7, land on E4 at bar 8 beat 1. The demonstration is not the completed assignment.
