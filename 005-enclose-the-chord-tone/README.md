## Give chromatic notes a destination

Choose a chord's third first. Play one semitone below it on beat 1, one semitone above it on the following &, then land on the target on beat 2. The short neighbours last 0.43 quarter-note beats each; the target lasts 0.88 beats. Leave the rest of the phrase open. The neighbours make sense because you can hear where they land. They do not each announce a new scale or mode.

## Exact lead notes and guitar positions

Middle C = C4 (MIDI 60). String B is the second string; e is the high first string.

| Chord | Below → above → third | Frets on one string |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C♯m | D♯4 → F4 → E4 | B: 4–6–5 |
| E | G4 → A4 → G♯4 | B: 8–10–9 |
| A | C5 → D5 → C♯5 | e: 8–10–9 |
| G♯7 | B4 → C♯5 → B♯4 | B: 12–14–13 |

B♯4 sounds like C5, but it is spelled B♯ because it is the major third of G♯7. In the fourth bar, G♯7 lasts beats 1–2, then C♯m returns on beat 3. The lead moves B♯4 to C♯5 at that return, holding C♯5 for 1.70 beats. Use B-string fret 14 for that C♯5.

## Harmony, bass and function

The four-bar phrase is C♯m | E | A | G♯7 (2 beats), C♯m (2 beats): i–bIII–bVI–V7–i. Each of the first three chords lasts a full bar. The separate bass follows roots, with no drone.

| Chord | Upper accompaniment | Bass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C♯m | E3–G♯3–C♯4 | C♯2 |
| E | E3–G♯3–B3 | E2 |
| A | E3–A3–C♯4 | A2 |
| G♯7 | F♯3–B♯3–D♯4 | G♯2 |

These accompaniment notes plus the bass form the named chords. The G♯7 upper part is a rootless seventh-chord voicing; the separate bass supplies G♯. Its B♯3 is MIDI 60.

## Demonstration timeline

- Bars 1–4: full four-bar phrase, neighbours and targets.
- Bars 5–8: same harmony and beat-2 targets, with only the two neighbour attacks removed. The cadence remains.
- Bars 9–12: C♯m only, repeating D♯4–F4–E4 to isolate the mechanism.
- Bars 13–16: full phrase, following each chord's third.
- Bars 17–20: hear it again, including B♯4 resolving to C♯5.
- Bars 21–24: four-bar preview of the writing task.

## Write eight bars

Use one chord per bar: C♯m–E–A–G♯7 | C♯m–F♯m–G♯7–C♯m. Target the thirds E, G♯, C♯, B♯, E, A, B♯, E. Put each arrival on beat 2 and keep it longer than its two approaches. In bar 7 resolve B♯ to C♯ as bar 8 begins, then arrive on E on beat 2. Keep the same rhythm while changing the targets; leave a rest after each arrival. Finally remove the neighbours and ask whether you can still hear the chord changes.
