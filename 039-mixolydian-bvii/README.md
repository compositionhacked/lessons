# Lesson 039 — Borrow ♭VII Without Losing the Tonic

**Goal:** Create a Mixolydian passage through ♭VII and ♭VII/I harmony, then choose whether the lead supplies the third or sustains the fourth.

**Lesson URL:** https://github.com/compositionhacked/lessons/tree/main/039-mixolydian-bvii

**Source:** CompositionHacked's *Fire Garden, Part II: Soloing and Modal Harmony* study, Mixolydian playbook and original Example F. Related recorded cases in the study: *Brother* uses B♭/C in C; *Hand on Heart* uses E/F♯ in F♯.

**Harmony and notes:** A Mixolydian is A B C♯ D E F♯ G. A is the tonic. G is ♭VII. G/A places G B D above A, with no C♯. B → C♯ supplies the tonic's major third; D is the fourth and a tone in G/A. Over G, C♯ is the ♯11 and is used as an approach to D.

**Demonstration:** A–C♯–E–D–C♯ over A; bend B to C♯ and sustain D over G/A; C♯–D–B–A–G over G, following Example F's tab.

**Eight-bar assignment (separate from the demo):** Program A | A | G/A | G/A | G | G | G/A | A. First hold D over G/A, resolving it to C♯ when A returns. Next use the B → C♯ bend against D in G/A. Over G, move C♯ quickly to D. Compare the suspended and third-supplied learner versions while keeping the backing fixed.

**Demo timing and assets:** 93 BPM, 4/4. The short MIDI demo spans seven total bars: one count-in and six musical bars (about 18.06 seconds) and presents the pictured three-bar phrase with repeated/held bars.
