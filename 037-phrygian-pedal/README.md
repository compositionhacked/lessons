# Lesson 037 — Write Phrygian Over an E Pedal

**Goal:** Establish E Phrygian with a tonic pedal, a ♭II upper structure, and purposeful note durations.

**Lesson URL:** https://github.com/compositionhacked/lessons/tree/main/037-phrygian-pedal

**Source:** CompositionHacked's *Fire Garden, Part II: Soloing and Modal Harmony* study, Phrygian playbook and original Example D. The study cites the *Taurus Bulba* Fmaj9♯11 harmony as an album case; this E5 → Fmaj7♯11/E illustration is original.

**Harmony and notes:** E Phrygian is E F G A B C D. E5 contains E and B. F is the ♭2 against E and works best as a short neighbor F → E over E5. Fmaj7♯11/E contains F A C E B above E; B is the chord's ♯11 and E's fifth.

**Demonstration:** Keep E in the bass. Play E–F–E–G–A–B over E5, then B(sustain)–C–B–A–F–E over Fmaj7♯11/E, as in Example D's tab.

**Eight-bar assignment (separate from the demo):** Make a continuous E-pedal loop and program E5 | E5 | Fmaj7♯11/E | Fmaj7♯11/E twice. First play only E and F over E5, always resolving F to E. Add the full melody, sustain B over the F chord, and check the E bass never drops out. Try Fmaj9♯11/E as a richer upper voicing after the basic move is clear.

**Demo timing and assets:** 92 BPM, 4/4. The short MIDI demo spans seven total bars: one count-in and six musical bars (about 18.26 seconds) and presents the pictured two-bar phrase with repeated/held bars.
