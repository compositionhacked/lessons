# Lesson 025 · Speed up the drums. Slow the lead.

[Download MIDI](025-demonstration.mid) · [Download MP3](025-demonstration.mp3)

96 BPM · 4/4

Hold E2 over Em. Move from eighth-note to sixteenth-note hi-hats while the lead moves through sixteenths and eighths to sustained G4 and E4.

Import the MIDI into Ableton at 96 BPM and assign instruments.

## Writing exercise

Write eight bars over Em. Double the drum subdivision at bar 5. Use sixteenths in bar 5, eighths in bar 6 and one held chord tone per bar in bars 7–8.

Independent virtual studio study of Queen’s The Miracle (1989). Original synthesized teaching audio. [Queen’s official album history](https://www.queenonline.com/news/the-miracle-35-years-on).
