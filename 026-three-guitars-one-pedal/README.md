# Lesson 026 · Stack three guitars over one pedal

[Download MIDI](026-demonstration.mid) · [Download MP3](026-demonstration.mp3)

104 BPM · 4/4

An E2 pedal anchors Em–D/E–C/E–D/E. From low to high, the upper parts are E3–G3–B3, D3–F♯3–A3, C3–E3–G3 and D3–F♯3–A3. Hear the top line first, then add the two lower voices in stereo over repeated E2.

Import the MIDI into Ableton at 104 BPM and assign instruments.

## Writing exercise

Write eight bars over E. Start with one melody and add two lower voices for bars 5–8. Resolve the voices into Em by the shortest available moves.

Independent virtual studio study of Queen’s The Miracle (1989). Original synthesized teaching audio. [Queen’s official album history](https://www.queenonline.com/news/the-miracle-35-years-on).
