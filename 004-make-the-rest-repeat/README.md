## Listen, then remove one beat

A fills all eight eighth-note positions with short guitar attacks. B deletes the attacks on 3 and the following &. Bass and a quiet hi-hat pulse keep playing. This is a guitar rest, not a full-band stop. Every retained attack has the same pitch, length, velocity and timbre in both versions.

Count: 1 & 2 & [3 & REST] 4 &. Each sounding chord lasts 0.27 quarter-note beats, followed by silence. The deliberate beat-3 gap lasts from beat 3 to beat 4. Mute both hands; do not fill it with a scrape. The hi-hat marks quarter notes, while the guitar plays straight eighths.

## Demonstration timeline

- Bars 1–4: A, Bb5 with every eighth position filled.
- Bars 5–8: B, the same Bb5 with beat 3 omitted.
- Bars 9–12: B repeated so you can count the gap.
- Bars 13–16: B over Bb5–Gb5–F5–Bb5, one chord per bar.
- Bars 17–18: A again; bars 19–20: B again.
- Bars 21–24: four-bar preview of B over the changing-root phrase.

The final four-bar preview is not the complete eight-bar assignment. All 24 bars are in both the MIDI and MP3, with section markers in the MIDI. There is no count-in.

## Write eight bars

Use Bb5–Bb5–Gb5–F5 | Bb5–Eb5–F5–Bb5. Keep the beat-3 guitar gap for bars 1–6. Make bars 7–8 answer it by moving one rest or changing the final attack. For Eb5 use x688xx = Eb3–Bb3–Eb4, with Eb2 in the bass. Keep bass and drums steady. Record both versions and ask someone to clap the gap before they hear it again.

Import the Type-1 MIDI at 112 BPM, assign a clean plucked guitar to channel 1, bass to channel 2, and a hi-hat to channel 10 (MIDI pitch 42). The Harmony track is intentionally empty in this lesson.
