# Lesson 032 · Turn every note into a major chord

[Download MIDI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/compositionhacked/lessons/main/032-fixed-major-harmony/032-demonstration.mid) · [Download MP3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/compositionhacked/lessons/main/032-fixed-major-harmony/032-demonstration.mp3)

104 BPM · 4/4

Hold E2. Play E4–F4–E♭4–E4–G4–B♭4–C♯5–E4, then let two extra voices double every note exactly four and seven semitones above. E4 produces E4–G♯4–B4. F4 produces F4–A4–C5. Every root gets the same major-triad shape. E major resolves against the E pedal.

In Ableton Live, set the project tempo to 104 BPM, then drag in the MIDI and assign instruments. Listen to the MP3 before editing.

## Writing exercise

Write eight bars over E2. Feed the harmonizer a semitone chain in bars 1–4 and a minor-third chain in bars 5–8. Finish on a major triad that contains E.
