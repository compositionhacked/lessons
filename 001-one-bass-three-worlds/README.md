# 001 — One bass note. Three worlds.

Hold a low E bass. Play Em, then E major, then F#/E.

G becoming G# changes the third; A# over E supplies the raised fourth. Let A# rise to B as the upper chord returns to E.

Write eight bars: two bars each of Em → E → F#/E → E, with E in the bass throughout.
