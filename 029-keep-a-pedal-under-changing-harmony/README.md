# Lesson 29 — Keep the bass. Move the harmony.

**Album study:** Dire Straits, *Communiqué* (1979) · **Song reference:** “Once Upon a Time in the West” · **Tempo:** 94 BPM · **Meter:** 4/4 · **Tuning:** standard guitar tuning.

> This is an original composition exercise about a device heard in the album. The audio is synthesized for teaching; it is not an album recording or a transcription. The eight-bar study is repeated once in the Reel.

## The device

A fixed D pedal is root, then fifth, then fifth. Bar 4 moves to A for the dominant. The bass creates continuity while the upper voicing changes.

## Eight-bar harmony

Dm · Bb/D · Gm/D · A7 · Dm · Cadd9 · Bbmaj7 · A7

## Writing assignment

Write eight bars over one low pitch. Change the upper harmony twice, then make one deliberate bass change at the cadence. Check every held note against each chord.
