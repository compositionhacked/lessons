# Lesson 028 · Turn one guitar into three

[Download MIDI](028-demonstration.mid) · [Download MP3](028-demonstration.mp3)

100 BPM · 4/4

Play E5–D5–C♯5–B4–A4–G♯4–F♯4–E4 in eighths. Add single repeats at 600 and 1,200 ms. On beat 3, A4 meets echoes C♯5 and E5. Use the printed MIDI echoes or set two zero-feedback, fully wet delays on separate returns.

Import the MIDI into Ableton at 100 BPM and assign instruments.

## Writing exercise

Write eight bars at 100 BPM. Compare stepwise eighths with stepwise sixteenths through the same delays. Plant notes in the final two beats of each chord that also fit the next one.

Independent virtual studio study of Queen’s The Miracle (1989). Original synthesized teaching audio. [Queen’s official album history](https://www.queenonline.com/news/the-miracle-35-years-on).
