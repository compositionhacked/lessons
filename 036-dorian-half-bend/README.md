# Lesson 036 — Make Dorian Audible with a Half-Bend

**Goal:** Make the Dorian 6th audible while resolving the half-step tension into the ♭7 of a minor-seventh chord.

**Lesson URL:** https://github.com/compositionhacked/lessons/tree/main/036-dorian-half-bend

**Source:** CompositionHacked's *Fire Garden, Part II: Soloing and Modal Harmony* study, Dorian playbook and original Example C. Recorded cases cited in the study: B → C in *The Crying Machine* verse; A♯ → B in its solo; G♯ → A in *Little Alligator*; F♯ → G in *Aching Hunger* (guitar-relative).

**Harmony and notes:** D Dorian is D E F G A B C. Dm7 is D F A C. B is Dorian's characteristic 6th, but it is not in Dm7 and rubs against C. G major (G B D), the major IV of D Dorian, contains B.

**Demonstration:** D–C–B(half-bend to C)–A, then G–F–A–B(half-bend to C), following Example C's tab. Tune the bend to C and release cleanly.

**Eight-bar assignment (separate from the demo):** Program Dm7 | Dm7 | Dm7 | Dm7 | G | G | Dm7 | Dm7. Put a B → C half-bend in every Dm7 phrase, varying its rhythmic placement. Sustain B over G, then return to short B → C bends when Dm7 resumes. Record and check both pitch and duration.

**Demo timing and assets:** 85 BPM, 4/4. The short MIDI demo spans seven total bars: one count-in and six musical bars (about 19.76 seconds) and presents the pictured two-bar phrase with repeated/held bars.
