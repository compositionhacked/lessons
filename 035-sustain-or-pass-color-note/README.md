# Lesson 035 — Hold the Color Note Where It Belongs

**Goal:** Decide note duration from the chord underneath, rather than treating a mode as permission to sustain every scale note.

**Lesson URL:** https://github.com/compositionhacked/lessons/tree/main/035-sustain-or-pass-color-note

**Source:** CompositionHacked's *Fire Garden, Part II: Soloing and Modal Harmony* study, Rule 2 and original Example B. The study cites the *There's a Fire in the House* plateau and *The Crying Machine* chorus as recorded cases of this principle.

**Harmony and notes:** A/G contains A, C♯ and E above a G bass. C♯ is its major third. Plain G contains G, B and D; C♯ sits a semitone below D and is a color note, not a chord tone.

**Demonstration:** Over A/G, play C♯–D–C♯ twice and sustain C♯. Over G, bend C♯ up a semitone to D; follow with G–B–D. The original tab is in Example B.

**Eight-bar assignment (separate from the demo):** Program A/G | A/G | G | G | A/G | A/G | G | G. Record one pass with a sustained C♯ on both chords, then a pass with the sustain only on A/G and C♯ → D over G. Compare how the G bar resolves. Mark chord notes and passing notes on your guide track.

**Demo timing and assets:** 82 BPM, 4/4. The short MIDI demo spans seven total bars: one count-in and six musical bars (about 20.49 seconds) and presents the pictured two-bar phrase with repeated/held bars.
